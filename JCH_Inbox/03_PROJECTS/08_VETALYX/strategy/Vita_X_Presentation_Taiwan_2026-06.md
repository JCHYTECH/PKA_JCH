## Slide 2 — The Problem We Solve

**Entering Europe is Harder Than It Looks.**

**You don't need a distributor. You need a European identity.**

### Stage 1 — Strategic Pre-Analysis

**Fixed cost: €10,000. Fixed duration: 4–6 weeks.**

A deep, multi-dimensional assessment of your product's European opportunity — grounded in data, regulation, and market reality.

**What we investigate:**

- **Market intelligence** — validated market sizing, competitor mapping, pricing corridors, distribution channels, country-by-country opportunity ranking. Not estimates. Double-checked data.
- **Regulatory pathway** — since veterinary product authorizations are granted at the *national* level in Europe, we map the exact registration requirements country by country, identify competent authorities, and document the full compliance dossier needed.
- **Clinical & scientific positioning** — which claims are defensible, which KOLs to engage, what evidence gaps to fill before launch.

**Deliverable:** A comprehensive Pre-Analysis Report containing:

1. **Market Data** — double-checked, source-verified, decision-ready
2. **Regulatory Documentation** — registration pathways, required technical files, timelines, and costs per target country
3. **Strategic Recommendation** — clear GO / NO-GO assessment with ranked market entry options and rationale
4. **Commercialization Timeline** — phased roadmap from Day 1 to first sale, with critical path identified

**Outcome:** You decide whether to proceed — and if so, *which path* to take. No obligation beyond Stage 1.

### Stage 2 — Full Nesting Execution

**Initiated only after your GO decision. Budgeted, phased, with STOP/GO gates.**

Once you commit, we build your European presence together — but never in one blind leap.

**Our commitment to you:**

- **Phased architecture** — the project is divided into discrete, budgeted phases (Structure → Validate → Launch → Scale), each with its own scope, deliverables, and fixed cost.
- **STOP/GO gates** — before each phase begins, you review the previous phase's results and decide whether to continue, pivot, or pause. Your capital is never locked into a runaway train.
- **Transparent budgeting** — every phase comes with a detailed estimate before it starts. No hidden costs. No scope creep without your approval.
- **Shared governance** — you remain in control of strategic decisions. We execute, recommend, and report — but you hold the steering wheel.

**Phase examples (illustrative):**
