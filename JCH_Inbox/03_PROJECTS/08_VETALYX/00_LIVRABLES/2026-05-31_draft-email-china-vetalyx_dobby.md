Dear Partners at DIAHO / ZHEDA DIXUN,  
亲爱的迪亚霍（DIAHO）/ 浙大迪迅（ZHEDA DIXUN）合作伙伴：

Following our recent discussions and the progress made on the European market launch, I am pleased to share with you several key documents for our strategic partnership.  
继我们近期关于欧洲市场启动的讨论及取得的进展之后，我很高兴与各位分享我们战略合作伙伴关系的几份关键文件。

### 1. Exclusive Distribution & Localization Contract  
### 1. 独家分销及本地化合同

You will find attached the draft for the **Exclusive Distribution, Regulatory Representation, and Industrial Localization** agreement for the European territory. This document establishes the long-term framework for our collaboration, including the "[[Start]]-up Phase" we discussed to ensure a successful launch in France and neighboring countries.  
随信附上针对欧洲地区的**独家分销、监管代表及工业本地化**协议草案。该文件确立了我们合作的长期框架，包括我们讨论过的"启动阶段"，以确保在法国及周边国家的成功启动。

### 2. Shareholders' Agreement & IP Equity Entry  
### 2. 股东协议及知识产权入股

Regarding the entry of DIAHO into the capital of VETALYX (40%), we have drafted a specific **Private Agreement**.  
关于迪亚霍入股 VETALYX（40%）的事宜，我们起草了一份专门的**私下协议书**。

As discussed, this 40% stake is guaranteed in exchange for the transfer of production trade secrets. We have added a standard **anti-dilution clause** to ensure fairness: the 40% participation is calculated based on the Founders' initial equity block and will be diluted by any external third-party investors who might join the project *prior* to the exercise of the option, in the same way the Founders' shares are diluted. This ensures a balanced capitalization table for all parties.  
诚如所议，这40%的股权是作为转让生产专有技术的对价而提供的。为确保公平，我们增加了一条标准的**反稀释条款**：这40%的持股比例是以创始人的初始股权块为基数计算的，并将按比例被在行使选择权*之前*可能加入项目的任何外部第三方投资者稀释，稀释方式与创始人股份相同。这确保了各方资本结构的平衡。

### 3. Usability Validation Report (Dr. Simon's Clinic, Liège)  
### 3. 可用性验证报告（列日Simon博士诊所）

We have successfully completed the first field usability tests at **Dr. Simon's veterinary clinic** in Liège, Belgium. The results are very encouraging:  
我们已在比利时列日的**Simon博士兽医诊所**成功完成了首批实地可用性试验。结果非常令人鼓舞：

- **Acceptability:** The clinical team found the kits very easy to use in [[daily]] routine.  
- **可用性：** 临床团队认为该套件在日常工作中非常易于使用。
- **Quality:** No analytical issues were reported.  
- **质量：** 未发现分析质量问题。
- **Optimization:** We identified small design adjustments for the pipette to improve handling with gloves.  
- **优化点：** 我们发现移液管需要进行微小的设计调整，以便于佩戴手套时操作。
- **Strategic Recommendation:** To ensure [[commercial]] success in Europe, we MUST adapt the **allergen composition** of the panels to meet European prevalence. Our report details the recommended 6-line panels for the EU market.  
- **战略建议：** 为确保在欧洲的商业成功，我们必须调整面板的**过敏原组成**，以符合欧洲的流行情况。我们的报告详细列出了针对欧洲市场的建议六线面板。
