## 1. Introduction | 1. 引言

This Quality Agreement defines the quality, regulatory, technical and communication responsibilities between Vetalyx and DiaHo for the supply of veterinary allergy diagnostic kits.

本质量协议界定 Vetalyx 与迪亚霍在供应兽用过敏诊断试剂盒方面各自承担的质量、法规、技术与沟通责任。

本协议所覆盖产品由迪亚霍开发、制造、实施质量控制并放行，并按照 Vetalyx 的产品命名、货号、商业外观与标签要求供应给 Vetalyx。

This Agreement does not replace any commercial, financial, supply, distribution, confidentiality, intellectual property or shareholder agreement between the Parties. 本协议不取代双方之间任何商业、财务、供应、分销、保密、知识产权或股东协议。如本质量协议与商业协议发生冲突，就质量与法规事项而言，以本质量协议的质量与法规条款为准，除非双方另有书面明确约定。

## 5. Definitions | 5. 定义

**Batch / Lot | 批次**

A defined quantity of product manufactured under essentially the same conditions and identified by a unique batch or lot number.

在基本相同条件下生产、并以唯一批号（或批次号）标识的一定数量的产品。

**Certificate of Analysis / CoA | 分析证书（CoA）**

A document issued by DiaHo confirming that a given batch has been tested and released according to approved specifications.

由迪亚霍出具的文件，确认某一批次已按照批准的规范完成检验并放行。

**Complaint | 投诉**

Any written, electronic or verbal communication alleging deficiencies related to the identity, quality, durability, reliability, safety, performance, packaging, labelling or usability of a product after release for distribution.

在产品放行分销后，就产品的特性、质量、耐用性、可靠性、安全性、性能、包装、标签或可用性提出缺陷指控的任何书面、电子或口头沟通。

**Deviation | 偏差**

Any departure from an approved procedure, specification, process parameter, instruction, test method or quality requirement that may affect product quality, safety, performance, traceability or regulatory compliance.

任何偏离经批准的程序、规范、工艺参数、指令、检验方法或质量要求、并可能影响产品质量、安全性、性能、可追溯性或法规合规性的情况。

**Non-conformity | 不合格**

Failure to fulfil a specified requirement.

未能满足规定要求的情形。

**Technical Documentation | 技术文档**

The documents and records required to demonstrate product design, intended use, performances (analytical and clinical), risk management, manufacturing control, quality control, stability, labelling, traceability and regulatory compliance.

用于证明产品设计、预期用途、性能（分析与临床）、风险管理、制造控制、质量控制、稳定性、标签、可追溯性及法规合规性所需的文件与记录。

**Vetalyx Nomenclature | Vetalyx 命名**

The product names, references, codes, packaging denomination and commercial identifiers defined by Vetalyx for the products covered by this Agreement.

Vetalyx 为本协议所覆盖产品定义的产品名称、货号、编码、包装名称及商业标识。
