# Executive Summary

Vetalyx is a Belgian‑incorporated diagnostic company focused on the
development, commercialization, and distribution of allergy diagnostic
solutions for companion animals. Location of Vetalyx is Marche en
Famenne Scientific-industrial area.

The company addresses a growing and structurally underserved veterinary
need: reliable, accessible, and clinically actionable allergy
diagnostics for dogs and cats. The initial product scope is strictly
limited to allergy diagnostics in order to ensure regulatory focus,
operational discipline, and rapid market entry.

Vetalyx targets veterinary clinics and veterinary diagnostic
distributors as its primary customers, positioning itself as a
pragmatic, science‑driven, and commercially realistic player rather than
a pure research organization. Other products for prevention and
treatment are part of the growing strategy

# 

# Company Overview and Vision

Vetalyx was established to bridge the gap between advanced immunological
diagnostics and everyday veterinary practice. While allergy prevalence
in companion animals continues to rise due to urbanization,
environmental exposure, and changes in pet lifestyles, diagnostic
practices remain fragmented, expensive, or insufficiently standardized.

Vetalyx long‑term vision is to become a trusted European reference in
veterinary allergy diagnostics by offering validated, scalable, and
regulatory‑compliant diagnostic kits that integrate seamlessly into
routine clinical workflows.

# 

# Problem Definition and Unmet Need

Veterinary practitioners frequently face uncertainty when diagnosing
allergic conditions in companion animals. Clinical symptoms are often
non‑specific, overlapping with parasitic, infectious, or dietary
disorders.

Existing diagnostic pathways are either indirect, slow, or
cost‑prohibitive. Many clinics rely on empirical treatment or exclusion
diets, leading to delayed diagnosis and suboptimal outcomes. Vetalyx
addresses this unmet need by providing targeted allergy diagnostics that
are reliable, rapid, and adapted to real‑world veterinary constraints.
The service includes the furniture of IT reader working in network if
requested.

# Product and Technology Description

Vetalyx initial product portfolio focuses on allergy diagnostic kits
designed to detect immunoglobulin E (IgE) responses to relevant
allergens in dogs and cats.

The technology platform supports both rapid lateral flow formats and
ELISA‑based assays, allowing flexibility in clinical use. The products
are intended for professional veterinary use only and are designed with
a strong emphasis on reproducibility, ease of use, and consistency
across batches. The platform includes automatic readers allowing easier
data management and network data exchanges.

Manufacturing is outsourced to a qualified supplier, allowing Vetalyx to
concentrate on validation, regulatory compliance, and market
development.

# Market Analysis

The companion animal diagnostics market in Europe is experiencing
sustained growth driven by increased pet ownership, humanization of
pets, and higher veterinary care spending.

France is selected as the lead market due to its large veterinary
population, structured distribution networks, and favorable regulatory
environment for veterinary diagnostics.

Beyond France, Vetalyx has identified Germany, Italy, Spain, and Belgium
as secondary expansion markets, offering a coherent regulatory and
commercial framework at the European level.

# Competitive Landscape and Positioning

The competitive landscape includes global diagnostic laboratories,
specialized veterinary diagnostic companies, and niche assay providers.
Many competitors focus either on centralized laboratory testing or on
broad diagnostic portfolios where allergy testing is not a core
priority. Veterinary customers are niche users , proud of their
activities and it is a marketing gain to have exclusive vet positioning
.

Vetalyx differentiates itself through a focused product scope, faster
turnaround times, and pricing designed to support routine clinical
adoption rather than occasional use. The company positions itself as
complementary rather than disruptive to existing diagnostic workflows.

# Regulatory Strategy

**Major Priority = complete technical file of Products must be available
rapidly with performances, stability ... data.**

Vetalyx operates under the European regulatory framework applicable to
veterinary in vitro diagnostic devices. This framework is more
administrative document providing than local evaluation and legal
paperworks a required for human products. The company has adopted a
proactive regulatory strategy, integrating compliance considerations
from product design through manufacturing and post‑market surveillance.

Belgium serves as the home market for initial regulatory
registration, with harmonized documentation supporting subsequent
European expansion.

Quality system implementation is progressive, with ISO alignment planned
later in line with commercial traction.

# Go‑to‑Market Strategy

The commercial strategy prioritizes indirect distribution through
established veterinary distributors, complemented by targeted direct
engagement with key opinion leader clinics. Pricing is positioned to
balance accessibility for clinics with sustainable margins. The initial
sales focus is on allergy‑focused veterinary practices and referral
clinics, gradually expanding to general practices as market familiarity
increases.

# Operations and Manufacturing Model

Manufacturing is fully outsourced to a single qualified supplier with
relevant experience in diagnostic kit production. This asset‑light model
minimizes capital expenditure and operational complexity. Vetalyx
retains responsibility for product specifications, validation protocols,
batch release criteria, and supplier oversight. Logistics and
warehousing are organized to support scalable European distribution.
Production localisation in Europe is an option exercised by the Board of Directors, part of short and
long-term strategy.

# Quality, Risk, and Compliance

Quality management is treated as a strategic asset rather than a
regulatory burden.

Vetalyx implements structured documentation, traceability, and complaint
handling processes from inception. Key risks include supplier
dependency, regulatory delays, and slower‑than‑expected market adoption.

Mitigation measures include contractual safeguards, phased market entry,
and conservative financial planning.

# Financial Logic and Funding Rationale

Vetalyx seeks external funding to support regulatory completion, initial
market entry, and working capital requirements.

The financial model prioritizes capital efficiency, with limited fixed
costs and a clear path to breakeven driven by volume growth rather than
price escalation.

Funding supports a multi‑year runway, allowing disciplined execution
without premature expansion.

# Milestones and Roadmap

Key milestones include regulatory clearance in Belgium (AFMPS/FAMHP), first commercial
sales, distributor onboarding, and progressive expansion into additional
European markets.

Product line extensions remain optional and are contingent on commercial
validation of the core allergy portfolio.

# Governance and Team

The company is governed by a structured board with relevant experience
in diagnostics and industrial projects.

Governance emphasizes oversight, risk control, and strategic alignment
rather than operational micromanagement.

External advisors support regulatory, scientific, and commercial
decision‑making.
