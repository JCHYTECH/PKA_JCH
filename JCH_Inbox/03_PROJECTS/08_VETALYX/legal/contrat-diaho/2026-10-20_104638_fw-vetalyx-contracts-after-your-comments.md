PART I
Preliminary Matter ― Working Language vs. Official Language
To ensure legal certainty in our exchanges, we propose that English be the official language of the framework agreement and its appendices. French and Chinese, assisted by machine translation, will remain working tools intended to facilitate mutual understanding.
For the Articles of Association (Statuts), however, the language is mandated by Belgian law: they must be drafted and filed with the notary in French. In this case, English and Chinese will only serve as working translations with no official legal standing.
Document 1 ― Articles of Association (Statuts)
We note with satisfaction that the provided text raised few comments. Your requested amendment regarding expense authorization appears well-founded, and we accept it.
Document 2 ― Commercial Framework Agreement
1. Governing Language (Article 40)
The language clause will be adapted in accordance with the preliminary matter stated above: English will become the official language of the contract, while French and Chinese will serve as working versions. This proposal is intended to prevent any potential misunderstandings.
2. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution (Article 38)
Since the company is incorporated in Belgium, Belgian law is applicable in the first instance. Applying Singaporean or Hong Kong law in conjunction with a Brussels jurisdiction would be unmanageable for our operations and is not acceptable to us. However, for disputes requiring mediation or arbitration, we gladly accept a seat in Switzerland(Geneva, for example).
3. Supply Terms ― Price Stability (Article 14)
We wish to guarantee the long-term price stability of our partnership. We propose to jointly define a mechanism to regulate the evolution of transfer prices (review period, indexation, arm's length principle). Could you please indicate how you plan to avoid price fluctuations without prior discussion, so that we can identify a common approach?
4. Definition of Related Companies (Articles 24 and 26, Appendix 10)
The links between Zheda Dixun and Diaho, which are clearly required and set out in the contract, appear appropriate to us. However, we wish to jointly clarify the definition of related companies and their reciprocal guarantees, particularly with regard to Appendix 10 (enhanced confidentiality and non-circumvention).
5. Authority of Vetalyx (in connection with Articles 24 and 26)
Furthermore, we want to ensure that Vetalyx retains full authority to enter into strategic commercial partnerships with non-Chinese companies, as this decision falls―as a reminder―under the purview of the Board of Directors.
6. Signature of Zheda Dixun
Since Zheda Dixun is explicitly named in the contract and is a shareholder of Diaho, would it not be wise for Dixun to also sign the agreement? This adjustment would clarify to third parties the respective roles of Diaho and Dixun, as well as their reciprocal guarantees.
Aside from these points, the other comments and amendments received do not raise any issues and are not deal-breakers; we propose to refine them during the next phase of discussions.
