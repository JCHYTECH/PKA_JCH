## PREAMBLE

**A.** DIAHO is a Chinese company that federates, coordinates and represents the products of four Chinese industrial partners — Zheda Dixun (principal partner), Hangzhou Darui (DX-Reader), Gangzhu and Zhunxin — in the field of veterinary diagnostics, in particular rapid tests for the detection of allergies in dogs and cats.

**B.** VETALYX holds commercial, scientific, regulatory and strategic expertise in the field of diagnostics and wishes to develop on the European market a network for the distribution, regulatory representation, registration, promotion and, where applicable, industrial localisation of the products federated by DIAHO.

**C.** **Strategic Preparation Assignment.** At DIAHO's request, VETALYX carries out a strategic preparation assignment relating to the establishment in Europe of the technical and commercial activities of Chinese companies in the diagnostics field, focusing primarily on DIAHO and its partners, but also open to other candidate Chinese companies. This Assignment is separate from the distribution relationship and is subject to specific remuneration (Article 6).

**D.** DIAHO warrants that it holds the rights, authorisations, licences and mandates necessary from its four Chinese partners to grant VETALYX the territorial exclusivity and the distribution, regulatory representation and industrial localisation rights provided for in this Agreement. DIAHO shall be solely liable towards VETALYX for any breach by a Chinese partner of its obligations, without prejudice to the direct undertakings of Zheda Dixun under Article 21.

**E.** **DIAHO's participation in the share capital.** The Parties agree that DIAHO shall join the share capital of VETALYX as a **founding shareholder holding forty percent (40%) upon incorporation** of the company, in return for a **cash contribution of one thousand six hundred euros (EUR 1,600)** against a subscribed share capital of ten thousand euros (EUR 10,000), of which four thousand euros (EUR 4,000) paid up at incorporation. The governance, anti-dilution, exit and balance payment terms shall be set out in a separate **Shareholders' Agreement**, which shall prevail for all share-capital matters.

**F.** **Characterisation of the relationships.** The Parties expressly acknowledge that DIAHO is at the same time (i) client of the Strategic Preparation Assignment, (ii) supplier of the Products and (iii) founding shareholder of VETALYX. This threefold relationship falls within the Belgian regime of **conflicts of interest** (Article 5:76 et seq. of the CSA) and requires compliance with the arm's length principle (Article 16).

It is hereby agreed as follows.

## Article 1 — Definitions

- **"Products"** : the veterinary diagnostic products federated by DIAHO, in particular rapid tests for the detection of allergies in dogs and cats (Annex 1).
- **"Initial Products"** : the first products described in Annex 1.
- **"Future Products"** : any new or improved veterinary diagnostic product developed by DIAHO, its Affiliates or its Chinese partners after the date of signature of this Agreement, falling within VETALYX's right of first refusal (Annex 8).
- **"Territory"** : the European continent, including the European Union and the other European countries (Annex 4).
- **"Regulatory Files"** : all files, registrations and authorisations established by VETALYX.
- **"Know-How"** : all technical, industrial and regulatory knowledge relating to the Products.
- **"Quality Agreement"** : the quality agreement entered into between the Parties (Annex 3), detailing quality responsibilities, batch release, traceability and change control.
- **"Assignment"** : the strategic preparation assignment defined in Article 6.
- **"Affiliate(s)"** : any company controlling, controlled by, or under common control with a Party, as well as the manufacturers and industrial partners federated by DIAHO — in particular **Zheda Dixun**, shareholder of DIAHO.
- **"Manufacturer"** : the actual manufacturer of the Products, namely **Zheda Dixun** for the Initial Products (or any manufacturer substituted or added with VETALYX's written consent), bound by the undertakings of Article 21.
- **"EU Authorised Representative"** : the legal person established in the European Union mandated by the manufacturer to carry out the tasks imposed by the applicable regulations, a mandate assumed by VETALYX (Article 8, Annex 6).
- **"Customer"** : any end user (veterinary clinic, laboratory, distributor) established in the Territory, in a commercial relationship with VETALYX for the Products.
- **"Agreement"** : this framework agreement and all its annexes.

## Article 2 — Purpose

The purpose of this Agreement is to define:

1. the **Strategic Preparation Assignment** entrusted by DIAHO to VETALYX (Article 6);
2. the **territorial exclusivity** for the distribution, promotion and registration of the Products (Article 3);
3. the **EU Authorised Representative mandate** (Article 8);
4. the **industrial localisation option** for the Products in Europe (Article 15);
5. the **right of first refusal** on Future Products (Annex 8);
6. the **direct undertakings of Zheda Dixun (Manufacturer)** towards VETALYX and DIAHO (Article 21).

This Agreement constitutes a strategic framework agreement and not a mere purchase-resale agreement.

---

## Article 3 — Territorial exclusivity

DIAHO grants VETALYX, for the entire term of the Agreement, total exclusivity over the Territory for the distribution, promotion, registration, regulatory representation and commercial development of the Products.

DIAHO undertakes not to sell or offer the Products in the Territory, directly or indirectly, otherwise than through VETALYX. Any request originating from the Territory shall be forwarded to VETALYX without delay.

**Scope of exclusivity.** The exclusivity covers the Products federated by DIAHO and its Affiliates. It does not in any way restrict VETALYX's ability to conclude, by decision of its Board of Directors, commercial or strategic partnerships with **non-Chinese companies** or concerning non-competing products, without prior information to or consent of DIAHO.

---

## Article 4 — No minimum sales target

The Parties expressly agree that the exclusivity granted to VETALYX is not subject to the achievement of any minimum sales volume, minimum turnover, minimum number of customers, minimum number of countries opened, or any binding commercial target. VETALYX undertakes to use reasonable efforts to develop the market for the Products in the Territory. No shortfall in sales volume may, on its own, justify termination of, a reduction of the Territory, or any challenge to the exclusivity, except in the case of a serious and demonstrated breach by VETALYX of its essential obligations.

---

## Article 5 — Term

The Agreement is entered into for an initial term of five (5) years, renewable for successive periods of three (3) years. Any Party wishing not to renew shall give written notice at least twelve (12) months before expiry.

Upon expiry or termination of the Agreement, for whatever reason, VETALYX may continue to sell the stocks of Products regularly acquired before the date of cessation for a period of twelve (12) months, except where prohibited by regulation or in the case of a proven product defect.

**Early termination.** Each Party may terminate the Agreement as of right in the event of a serious breach by the other Party of its essential obligations, after a formal notice that has remained without effect for a period of thirty (30) days.

The following constitute in particular serious breaches by DIAHO:

1. breach of exclusivity;
2. circumvention of VETALYX;
3. direct or indirect sale in the Territory;
4. refusal to provide the necessary regulatory documents;
5. provision of false or seriously incomplete technical or regulatory information;
6. a significant and uncorrected deterioration in the quality of the Products;
7. breach of the confidentiality obligations;
8. breach of the right of first refusal.

The following constitute in particular serious breaches by VETALYX:

1. persistent non-payment of sums due;
2. serious breach of the confidentiality obligations;
3. unauthorised use of DIAHO's Know-How outside the Agreement;
4. a serious regulatory fault directly attributable to VETALYX.

## Article 6 — Strategic Preparation Assignment

**6.1. Scope of the Assignment.** VETALYX carries out a strategic preparation assignment relating to the establishment in Europe of the technical and commercial activities of Chinese companies in the diagnostics field, focusing primarily on DIAHO and its industrial partners, and open to other candidate Chinese companies.

**6.2. Scope of services.** The Assignment includes in particular: analysis of the European veterinary diagnostics market; identification of regulatory requirements (AFMPS/FAMHP and the European regulatory framework applicable to veterinary diagnostic devices); market-entry and commercial structuring strategy; identification of partners, channels and experts; preparation of registration files; establishment of a **serum bank** and coordination of the **clinical validation studies** of the Products.

**6.3. Remuneration.** In consideration of the Assignment, DIAHO shall pay VETALYX:

1. a fixed amount of **one hundred thousand euros excluding taxes (EUR 100,000)** in respect of the strategic preparation assignment;
2. an additional amount of **twenty thousand euros excluding taxes (EUR 20,000)** in respect of the clinical validation studies and the establishment of the serum bank.

Being a total of **one hundred and twenty thousand euros excluding taxes (EUR 120,000)**.

**6.4. Payment terms.** The invoicing and payment schedule (advance, milestones, deadlines, currency, taxes) shall be set out in a separate written agreement or in Annex 5. In the absence of any agreement to the contrary, the amounts are invoiced in euros and payable within thirty (30) days.

**6.5. Ownership of deliverables.** The deliverables of the Assignment (studies, strategies, serum bank, preparatory files) are the exclusive property of VETALYX, subject to the pre-existing intellectual property of DIAHO and its partners.

**6.6. Independence.** The Assignment is an independent service, separate from the distribution relationship and from DIAHO's participation in the share capital.

## Article 9 — Quality and Quality Agreement

The Products are manufactured in accordance with the applicable specifications and the **Quality Agreement** (Annex 3).

## Article 10 — Pricing and logistics

The transfer prices are set out in Annex 5, in accordance with the arm's length principle (Article 16).

**Price stability.** The Parties undertake to guarantee the stability of the transfer prices over time. To this end, they agree on a **pricing mechanism** comprising:

1. a fixed **review period** (by default: twelve (12) months);
2. an **indexation**, where applicable, based on an objective and published index, agreed in Annex 5;
3. the **impossibility for DIAHO to increase prices unilaterally without prior discussion**: any change requires **six (6) months' prior written notice**, an **objective justification** and the **written agreement** of the Parties.

Transactions are denominated in euros (EUR). No minimum order quantity applies during the first twenty-four (24) months.

**Launch stock.** In order to support the commercial launch, DIAHO shall provide VETALYX, without initial invoicing and without immediate payment, with diagnostic kits for a total value of fifty thousand euros excluding taxes (EUR 50,000), calculated on the basis of the transfer price applicable between the Parties. These kits are provided as an initial commercial launch stock; their reimbursement by VETALYX to DIAHO shall be exclusively progressive and conditional upon sales actually made by VETALYX in the Territory. Unless otherwise agreed in writing between the Parties:

1. VETALYX shall progressively reimburse the value of the kits by offsetting against sales actually collected;
2. no reimbursement shall be due as long as the Products concerned have not generated actual sales;
3. in the absence of sales, DIAHO may not claim from VETALYX any reimbursement, payment, indemnity or compensation in respect of the kits provided for the launch;
4. the schedule and the percentage of reimbursement per sale shall be defined in Annex 5 or by separate written agreement;
5. the kits provided in this context may be used by VETALYX for demonstrations, commercial validation, customer trials, training, veterinary evaluation, promotion, constitution of initial stock or first sales, according to the strategy decided by VETALYX.

## Article 21 — Undertakings of Zheda Dixun (Manufacturer)

**21.1. Role and scope.** Zheda Dixun acts herein as the **actual Manufacturer** of the Initial Products and as shareholder of DIAHO. By signing this Agreement and/or the **Deed of Accession** (Annex 10), Dixun **adheres** to the obligations set out in this Article 21 as well as in Articles 8, 9, 12, 13 and 15 and in Annex 9, to the extent they concern it. These undertakings are **directly enforceable** by VETALYX and by DIAHO.

**21.2. Quality and conformity.** Dixun warrants that the Products are manufactured in accordance with the applicable specifications (Annex 2), the Quality Agreement (Annex 3) and the Chinese and European regulatory requirements applicable to manufacturing. Dixun shall apply change control and shall refrain from any modification of the design, composition, manufacture or performance of the Products without VETALYX's prior written consent.

**21.3. Regulatory and technical documentation.** Dixun shall provide — directly or through DIAHO — all data, files, certificates and technical documentation necessary for the regulatory registrations of the Products in the Territory (Article 8). It warrants the accuracy, truthfulness and completeness thereof.

**21.4. Non-circumvention and exclusivity.** Dixun undertakes not to sell, offer or supply, directly or indirectly, the Products in the Territory otherwise than through VETALYX. It shall forward without delay to VETALYX and DIAHO any request or order originating from the Territory.

**21.5. Confidentiality.** Dixun is bound by the confidentiality obligations of Article 12 and the enhanced confidentiality of Annex 9.

**21.6. Continuity of supply and price stability.** Dixun undertakes to ensure continuity of supply of the Products ordered by VETALYX and to comply with the pricing mechanism of Article 10, without unilateral increase.

**21.7. Audit.** Dixun accepts the audits of Article 13 and cooperates in good faith, in particular by granting access to its manufacturing sites, its quality system and its documentation.

**21.8. Industrial localisation.** Dixun shall cooperate with the transfer of Know-How and the industrial localisation provided for in Article 15 and Annex 7, and shall grant, where applicable, the intellectual property licences necessary for this purpose, under a separate agreement. Without prejudice to the commercial terms to be agreed, this clause entails neither assignment nor an automatic licence.

**21.9. Joint and several liability.** For the obligations referred to in this Article 21, DIAHO and Dixun are **jointly and severally liable** towards VETALYX. Dixun's undertakings do not release DIAHO from its own obligations; DIAHO remains fully liable towards VETALYX, including in the event of a breach by Dixun. No dispute between DIAHO and Dixun, and no provision of their internal agreements, shall be enforceable against VETALYX.

**21.10. Term.** Dixun's undertakings follow the term of the Agreement. Upon expiry of the Agreement, the **non-circumvention** obligations (Articles 12 and 21.4) shall survive for five (5) years, and the **confidentiality** obligations (Articles 12 and 21.5) shall survive **without limitation in time** for as long as the information concerned remains confidential.

### Annex 7 — Know-how transfer and industrial localisation
This Annex defines the framework for progressively transferring to Europe all or part of the activities relating to Product preparation, assembly, packaging, control or manufacture.

Under Article 15, VETALYX may exercise the localisation option by decision of its Board. The Parties shall have eight (8) months from notification to organise the Know-How transfer. The option does not itself require an immediate industrial transfer: implementation of each stage requires an agreement defining scope, timetable, responsibilities, costs and rights to use the relevant Know-How. European localisation may include:
- document transfer and training;
- assembly, packaging, labelling and adaptation of instructions;
- quality control and batch release;
- partial or complete production and industrial validation;
- selection of European partners;
- Know-How protection, indicative timetable, Parties' responsibilities and any cost sharing.

Each transferred activity shall be validated before commercial implementation to demonstrate that Product characteristics, quality and performance remain consistent with the approved specifications.

### Annex 9 — Enhanced confidentiality and non-circumvention
This Annex supplements the Agreement's confidentiality, exclusivity and non-circumvention provisions. It protects information, commercial relationships and opportunities developed through the partnership, particularly VETALYX's investment in identifying, developing and structuring the European market.

**Confidentiality.** DIAHO shall not disclose or use outside the Agreement the technical, regulatory, commercial, financial or strategic information communicated by VETALYX.

**Protected contacts and commercial relationships.** Customers, prospects, distributors, partners, experts and other contacts identified, introduced or developed by VETALYX under the Agreement are Protected Contacts. DIAHO shall not establish a commercial relationship aimed at bypassing VETALYX or use those relationships to conclude agreements directly or indirectly with such contacts.

**Regulatory Files and territorial sales.** Files, translations, adaptations and regulatory work created or financed by VETALYX shall not be used for a third party's benefit in the Territory without VETALYX's written consent. DIAHO shall not make direct or indirect territorial sales aimed at bypassing VETALYX's exclusivity.

**Affiliates, partners and guarantees.** DIAHO shall ensure compliance by its parent companies, subsidiaries, Affiliates, Zheda Dixun, partner manufacturers, agents and relevant distributors. Dixun's direct obligations and DIAHO/Dixun's joint and several liability remain governed by Article 21.9; their internal arrangements shall not be enforceable against VETALYX.

**Breach and existing rights.** A substantial breach constitutes a serious contractual breach and may require immediate cessation, compensation for loss and, where appropriate, termination under Article 5. Article 21.10 governs survival of Dixun's non-circumvention and confidentiality obligations. The Parties' pre-existing rights in information, technology, contacts and commercial relationships are preserved, without authorising a breach of the Agreement's exclusivity.

If this Annex conflicts with general confidentiality or non-circumvention provisions, the Parties shall expressly clarify precedence by written agreement.
