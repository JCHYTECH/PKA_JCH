## PREAMBLE

This Shareholders' Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between the five (5) founding shareholders of VETALYX SRL (the "Company"), a Belgian private limited liability company whose registered office is established at Novalis Science Park, Rue de la Science, 6900 Marche-en-Famenne.

VETALYX's vocation is to become a reference European hub in the sector of rapid veterinary diagnostics, food supplements, medicinal products and related services, by combining operational expertise and the capacity to host international partners wishing to establish themselves on the European market.

The Parties share the vision of an evolution in three phases: **(1)** operational consolidation and the first profitability threshold; **(2)** successive capital increases and the admission of third-party investors; **(3)** the conclusion of representation and distribution agreements with foreign companies using VETALYX as a vehicle for entry onto the European market.

DIAHO (a company governed by Chinese law) enters the capital **upon incorporation** as a founding shareholder to the extent of **40 %**, by way of a **cash** contribution. The four European founders collectively hold **60 %** (4 × 15 %).

The purpose of this Agreement is to govern governance, dilution protection, transfers of shares and the exit of the Shareholders, in the common interest of the Company.

## TITLE II — GOVERNANCE

### Article 4 — Board of directors

The Company is administered by a board of directors (Articles of Association, Art. 14). The Shareholders agree on the following arrangements:

- The board is composed of **two (2) directors**, **designated by the European founders** (through the Founders' Block Agreement).
- **DIAHO is not a director**: it designates an **observer** to the board of directors, pursuant to Article 4 bis.
- Any expenditure exceeding **10 000 euros** requires the signature of two (2) directors; any expenditure exceeding **30 000 euros** requires the approval of the board (Articles of Association, Art. 15).

### Article 4 bis — Observer to the board of directors

DIAHO, in its capacity as shareholder, designates an **observer** to the board of directors, in the person of **Xu Wenjun (Céline)**, or of any other representative that it may substitute. It is agreed as follows:

1. **Designation and replacement** — DIAHO may designate one or more observers and replace them at any time, by simple written notice to the chairman of the board, without any approval formality.
2. **Participation** — the observer attends the meetings of the board of directors and receives, within the same timeframes as the directors, the agenda and the preparatory documents.
3. **Information** — the observer receives the minutes of the meetings and the financial information communicated to the directors.
4. **Expression** — the observer may ask questions, formulate observations and submit documents "for review" to the board; the chairman allocates speaking time and everything is recorded in the minutes.
5. **Non-deliberative** — the observer **has no deliberative vote and no right of veto**. He/she does not take part in the votes and holds **no management or representation power** over the Company.
6. **Absence of mandate** — the observer **does not have the status of director**, does not bind the Company and bears no director's liability. Consequently, the regimes of the permanent representative (CSA, Art. 2:55) and of conflicts of interest (CSA, Art. 5:76) do not apply to him/her.
7. **Confidentiality** — the observer is bound, to the same extent as the directors, to the strictest confidentiality over all information received in that capacity.
8. **Disagreement** — any written observation or disagreement of the observer is recorded in the minutes. It has evidentiary value, without suspending or preventing the board's decision.
9. **Limitation** — the observer intervenes only in an information and advisory capacity, without co-management; he/she may in no case be characterised as a de facto director.

DIAHO's shareholder rights (in particular Article 5) remain intact and independent of the present observation.

### Article 5 — Reserved decisions

**5.1 — Decisions at a majority of 51 %.** The following decisions are taken by a majority of Shareholders representing at least **fifty-one per cent (51 %)** of the capital. Each Shareholder undertakes, under a voting agreement, to vote accordingly and not to use a blocking minority to oppose them:

1. Amendment of the corporate purpose (Articles of Association, Art. 5) — except amendments required by the authorities, which are not opposable;
2. Transfer of the registered office outside Belgium (Articles of Association, Art. 3);
3. Transfer or contribution of the whole or of an essential branch of the activity;
4. Creation of subsidiaries or branches (Articles of Association, Art. 6).

**5.2 — DIAHO protective veto.** By way of exception, the decision to contract **indebtedness exceeding 175 000 euros** outside ordinary management requires the **favourable vote of DIAHO**. DIAHO holds no other right of veto.

This article does not modify the legal or statutory majorities applicable to the validity of the corporate decision; it creates a contractual voting obligation between the Shareholders (except 5.2).

### Article 6 — Reserved decisions — Founders' Block

The four founders agree to deliberate and vote **as a block** on the following decisions (protection of the 60 %), through the Founders' Block Agreement (separate document — layer 2):

1. Appointment and dismissal of the directors designated by the founders.
2. Admission of any new shareholder (approval, cf. Art. 11).
3. Adoption of the annual strategic plan and budget.
4. Exercise of the buy-back option in the event of the exit of a founder.

### Article 7 — Founders' Committee

A Founders' Committee (the four founders) meets at least once per quarter to prepare the block's positions and to arbitrate internal disagreements. It binds the Company only within the limits of the powers delegated by the board.

### Article 8 — Conflicts of interest

Any Shareholder in a situation of conflict of interest, direct or indirect, with a decision submitted to the board or to the general meeting must inform the other Shareholders without delay and abstain from voting (Articles of Association, Art. 18 for directors; CSA, Art. 5:76-5:78).

**Specific DIAHO provision**: as DIAHO is both shareholder and commercial counterparty of the Company (framework agreement, strategic preparation mission), any agreement between the Company and DIAHO (or its affiliates) is subject to the prior authorisation of the board, **DIAHO abstaining from voting** on such agreement. The terms (price, exclusivity) are documented and brought to the general meeting in the management report.

## TITLE III — TRANSFERS OF SHARES

### Article 9 — Lock-up

For a period of **three (3) years** from the signature of the Agreement, no Shareholder may transfer, contribute, pledge or otherwise dispose of all or part of its shares without the unanimous consent of the other Shareholders.

### Article 10 — Right of first refusal

Upon expiry of the lock-up, any Shareholder wishing to transfer its shares notifies its intention to the other Shareholders (registered letter), indicating the number of shares, the price, the identity of the transferee and the terms. The other Shareholders have **thirty (30) days** to exercise their right of first refusal, pro rata to their participation. Order of priority: founders (as a block) → DIAHO → third parties.

### Article 11 — Approval (agrément)

Any transfer to a non-Shareholder third party is subject to the approval of the Shareholders representing **at least 50 % of the capital**. In the event of refusal, the remaining Shareholders purchase the shares within a period of **three (3) months**; failing which, the approval is deemed granted.

### Article 12 — Tag-along / Drag-along

- **Tag-along**: in the event of a transfer of more than 50 % of the capital by a Shareholder to a third party, the other Shareholders have a right of joint exit on the same terms.
- **Drag-along**: the Shareholders together representing at least **75 %** of the capital may compel the minority Shareholders to transfer their shares on the same terms as a firm offer from a third party acting in good faith.

### Article 13 — Exclusion

A Shareholder may be excluded in the following exhaustive cases, upon decision of the Shareholders representing at least **50 % of the votes** (the Shareholder concerned not taking part in the vote):

1. Final criminal conviction adversely affecting commercial reputability.
2. Serious and repeated breach of the Agreement, not remedied within thirty (30) days of formal notice.
3. Direct unfair competition.
4. For DIAHO specifically: substantial breach of the framework agreement, or sanction under ISC/UBO.

The buy-back is carried out pursuant to Article 14, with a discount of **15 %**.

### Article 14 — Valuation of shares

In the event of a forced transfer (exclusion, death, incapacity) or of a disagreement on the price, the value of the shares is determined by a combination of:

- **EBITDA** (weighting 40 %): sector multiple applied to the gross operating result of the last twelve months;
- **Net asset value** (weighting 30 %): revalued net assets;
- **DCF** (weighting 30 %): discounted cash flows over five years.

**Pre-revenue floor** (revenue < 100 000 euros over twelve months): revalued net assets, multiple between 1 and 3, never below the paid-up capital increased by the current-account contributions.

## TITLE IV — CAPITAL INCREASES

### Article 15 — Preferential subscription right

Upon any capital increase, each Shareholder has a preferential subscription right in order to maintain its percentage. Notification forty-five (45) days in advance, exercise within twenty (20) days, transferable between Shareholders before being offered to third parties.

### Article 15 bis — Decision on capital increase

1. The Shareholders agree that any decision to increase the capital, to create or issue new shares, or to issue any instrument giving access to the capital, is deemed approved as soon as it obtains the agreement of Shareholders together representing at least **fifty-one per cent (51 %)** of the capital.

2. Each Shareholder irrevocably undertakes, under a voting agreement, to vote in favour of any capital increase so approved, whether at a general meeting or in writing, and not to use its voting right or any blocking minority to oppose it.

3. This article does not modify the legal or statutory majorities applicable to the validity of the corporate decision. In the event of breach, the defaulting Shareholder is answerable pursuant to Articles 13 (exclusion) and 20 (bad leaver) of this Agreement.

## TITLE V — SHAREHOLDERS' UNDERTAKINGS

### Article 16 — Non-compete

Each Shareholder undertakes, for the entire duration of its participation and for **one (1) year** following its exit, not to carry on, directly or indirectly, an activity competing with that of the Company within the European territory.

### Article 17 — Confidentiality

The Shareholders treat as strictly confidential all information relating to the Company, its IP, its partners and its financial situation. This obligation survives the Shareholder's exit for **three (3) years**.

### Article 18 — Minimum involvement

Each Shareholder undertakes to devote the time and attention necessary to its mission and to participate in the meetings of the board and of the Founders' Committee.

---

## TITLE VI — EXIT, DEATH, DEPARTURE

### Article 19 — Death and incapacity

In the event of death, the Company continues with the surviving Shareholders and the heirs, subject to approval (Articles of Association, Art. 12). In the event of refusal, the shares are purchased pursuant to Article 14 within six (6) months. The Company takes out "key person" insurance within thirty (30) days of signature.

### Article 20 — Good Leaver / Bad Leaver

- **Good Leaver** (departure for personal or health reasons, or by mutual agreement): buy-back at **100 %** of the value (Art. 14).
- **Bad Leaver**: discount of **25 %** (departure in breach of the Agreement, without characterised misconduct) or **50 %** (gross misconduct, misappropriation, unfair competition).

### Article 21 — Change of control of DIAHO

Any change of control of DIAHO (acquisition of DIAHO by a third party, transfer of the majority of its capital) is subject to the **prior approval** of the other Shareholders. Failing such approval, the other Shareholders have a priority buy-back right over DIAHO's shares pursuant to Article 14.

## TITLE VII — DEADLOCK AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### Article 22 — Conciliation and mediation

In the event of a dispute relating to the Agreement, the Parties seek an amicable solution within thirty (30) days, then resort to a mediator appointed by mutual agreement or, failing that, by the President of the Enterprise Court.

### Article 23 — Persistent deadlock — Buy-Sell (Shotgun)

In the event of a persistent deadlock exceeding **ninety (90) days** on a critical decision, either of the two blocks (DIAHO block vs Founders' block) may trigger the following procedure:

1. The requesting block sets a **price per share** and notifies it to the opposing block.
2. The opposing block has **thirty (30) days** to choose, exclusively: **(a) to purchase** the shares of the requesting block at that price, or **(b) to sell** its own shares to the requesting block at that same price.
3. **Grace period** — if the opposing block chooses to **purchase**, the price is payable against a **10 % deposit** paid upon exercise of the option, the balance being payable no later than within **twelve (12) months**, bearing interest at the statutory rate. During this period, the shares of the requesting block are **unavailable** (inalienable and not pledged).
4. Failing full payment at maturity, the requesting block may, at its choice, demand forced execution or exercise the reverse option by purchasing the shares of the opposing block at the same price.
5. The Founders' block's response is decided pursuant to the **Founders' Block Agreement** (Annex 1).

Last-resort mechanism.

### Article 24 — Arbitration

Failing an amicable resolution, any dispute is submitted to **ICC** arbitration, in accordance with the arbitration rules of the International Chamber of Commerce, seated in **Geneva (Switzerland)**, in the French language.

## TITLE VIII — INFORMATION AND REPORTING

### Article 25 — Reporting and information rights

The Company provides each Shareholder with:

- **Quarterly**: cash position, commercial dashboard, management accounts, reporting of representation agreements. Switching to **monthly** as from an annual revenue of 100 000 euros.
- **Annually**: final accounts, status of ongoing agreements, prospectus.

All Shareholders have the same information rights, including access to the management accounts and to the indicators of the strategic preparation mission. Any Shareholder may question the director, who answers within ten (10) days.

## TITLE IX — FINAL PROVISIONS

### Article 26 — Duration and amendment

The Agreement is entered into for an indefinite duration. It terminates upon dissolution of the Company or by unanimous written consent of the Shareholders. Any amendment requires unanimous consent, formalised by a signed addendum.

### Article 27 — Entire agreement and severability

The Agreement and its annexes constitute the entire agreement on its subject matter and supersede any prior agreement on the same subject matter. If any provision is declared null, the others remain in force; the Parties negotiate in good faith a substitute provision achieving the same economic purpose.

### Article 28 — Governing law and jurisdiction

The Agreement is governed by Belgian law. Failing arbitration (Art. 24), exclusive jurisdiction of the Belgian enterprise courts.

### Article 29 — Notices

Any notice is given by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt to the addresses indicated on the signature page, or by any means establishing the date of receipt with certainty.

### Article 30 — Hierarchy with the Articles of Association

The Agreement prevails between the Shareholders in the event of contradiction with the Articles of Association (Articles of Association, Art. 32). The Agreement is not enforceable against third parties.
