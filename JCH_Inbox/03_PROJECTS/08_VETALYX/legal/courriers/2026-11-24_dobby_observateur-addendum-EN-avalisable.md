## RECITALS

**WHEREAS** the Shareholders have entered into a Shareholders' Agreement dated 5 October 2026 (the "Agreement");

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Shareholders agree as follows:

---

## 1. Amendment to Article 4 — Board of directors

Article 4 of the Agreement is amended and restated as follows:

> The Company is administered by a board of directors (Articles of Association, Art. 14). The Shareholders agree on the following arrangements:
>
> - The board is composed of **two (2) directors**, **designated by the European founders** (through the Founders' Block Agreement).
> - **DIAHO is not a director**: it designates an **observer** to the board of directors, pursuant to Article 4 bis.
> - Any expenditure exceeding **10 000 euros** requires the signature of two (2) directors; any expenditure exceeding **30 000 euros** requires the approval of the board (Articles of Association, Art. 15).

---

## 2. New Article 4 bis — Observer to the board of directors

The following Article 4 bis is inserted immediately after Article 4:

> DIAHO, in its capacity as shareholder, designates an **observer** to the board of directors, in the person of **Xu Wenjun (Céline)**, or of any other representative that it substitutes. The following is agreed:
>
> 1. **Designation and replacement** — DIAHO may designate one or more observers and replace them at any time, by simple written notice to the chairman of the board, without any approval formality.
> 2. **Attendance** — the observer attends the meetings of the board of directors and receives, within the same time limits as the directors, the agenda and the preparatory documents.
> 3. **Information** — the observer receives the minutes of the meetings and the financial information communicated to the directors.
> 4. **Expression** — the observer may ask questions, formulate observations and submit documents "for examination" to the board; the chairman allocates speaking time and all of the above is recorded in the minutes.
> 5. **Non-deliberative** — the observer **has no deliberative vote and no right of veto**. He or she does not take part in the votes and holds **no management power and no power to represent** the Company.
> 6. **No mandate** — the observer **does not have the status of director**, does not bind the Company and bears no director's liability. Consequently, the regimes of the permanent representative (CSA, Art. 2:55) and of conflicts of interest (CSA, Art. 5:76) do not apply to him or her.
> 7. **Confidentiality** — the observer is bound, to the same extent as the directors, to the strictest confidentiality with respect to all information received in that capacity.
> 8. **Objection** — any written observation or objection of the observer is recorded in the minutes. It has probative value, without suspending or preventing the decision of the board.
> 9. **Scope** — the observer intervenes only in an information and advisory capacity, without co-management; he or she may in no case be qualified as a de facto director.
>
> The shareholder rights of DIAHO (in particular Article 5) remain intact and independent of this observation.
