Following the Articles of Association, I am pleased to share the summary of the **Shareholders' Agreement** (version 4, dated 24 November 2026), which finalises the governance of VETALYX.

Key terms:

1. **Board of directors: 2 directors** — Alain Bosseloir and Jean-Claude Havaux, designated by the European founders. **DIAHO is not a director** and participates through an **observer** (Xu Wenjun / Céline), attending board meetings **without a deliberative vote and without management power** (Articles 4 and 4 bis).

2. **Reserved decisions:** taken at a **51 %** majority of the capital (corporate purpose, transfer of the registered office outside Belgium, transfer of an essential branch, creation of subsidiaries). DIAHO retains a **single protective veto, limited to indebtedness exceeding €175,000** outside ordinary management (Article 5).

3. **Capital:** €4,000 fully paid up at incorporation (Article 2).

4. **Transfers of shares:** 3-year lock-up, right of first refusal, 50 % approval for third parties, tag-along, and drag-along at **75 %** (Articles 9–12).

5. **Capital increases:** decided at a **51 %** majority under a voting agreement (Article 15 bis).

6. **Exit:** good leaver / bad leaver provisions, and prior approval for any change of control of DIAHO (Articles 19–21).

7. **Deadlock resolution:** conciliation and mediation, then a buy-sell (shotgun) mechanism with a 12-month grace period, and finally **ICC arbitration seated in Geneva** (Articles 22–24).
