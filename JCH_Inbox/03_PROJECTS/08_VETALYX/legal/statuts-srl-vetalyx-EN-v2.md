## TITLE I — FORM, NAME, REGISTERED OFFICE, DURATION, PURPOSE

### Article 1 — Form

The company is incorporated as a Belgian private limited liability company (*société à responsabilité limitée*, SRL), governed by the Companies and Associations Code (CSA).

### Article 2 — Name

The company is named « VETALYX ». In all deeds, invoices, notices, publications and other documents issued by the company, the corporate name must be preceded or followed by the indication « SRL », together with the registered office and the company number with the Crossroads Bank for Enterprises (BCE).

### Article 3 — Registered office

The registered office is established at Novalis Science Park, Rue de la Science, 6900 Marche-en-Famenne.

It may be transferred to any other place in the Kingdom by decision of the board of directors, without amendment of these articles of association.

### Article 4 — Duration

The company is incorporated for an unlimited duration. It may be dissolved at any time by decision of the extraordinary general meeting.

### Article 5 — Corporate purpose

The company's purpose, in Belgium and abroad, on its own behalf or on behalf of third parties, is:

**Main activities :**

1. Research, development, manufacture, distribution and marketing of all products, equipment, consumables, reagents and instruments intended for veterinary diagnostics, in particular rapid allergy detection tests for companion animals, livestock and equids.
2. Research, development, formulation, distribution and marketing of food supplements for animals, veterinary dietetic products, nutraceuticals.
3. Research, development, import, distribution and marketing of veterinary medicinal products, veterinary medical devices and animal health products.
4. Provision of veterinary services: consulting, training, audit, expertise, clinical research, screening, telemedicine.
5. Regulatory representation of foreign manufacturers on the European markets (REP mandate, registrations, pharmacovigilance).
6. Conclusion of strategic partnerships: exclusive distribution, licensing, franchising, joint ventures.

**Related activities :**

7. Real estate investment: acquisition, holding, management, rental and disposal of real property.
8. Acquisition of participations in any Belgian or foreign companies.
9. Management of subsidiaries and provision of administrative, financial, commercial and technical services.
10. Granting of guarantees and security interests for related companies.
11. Consulting, study, research and training in the fields related to the corporate purpose.
12. Sweep-up clause: all operations directly or indirectly related to the corporate purpose.

### Article 6 — French subsidiary

Planned creation of a subsidiary under French law. Its form, name, registered office and purpose are determined by an extraordinary general meeting convened by the board of directors in due course.

The share capital is fixed at **10 000 euros** (ten thousand euros), represented by 10 000 shares without nominal value. Upon incorporation, **4 000 shares are fully paid up** (4 000 euros). The remaining 6 000 shares will be paid up upon call by the board of directors. The capital may be increased or reduced by decision of the extraordinary general meeting in accordance with the CSA.

### Article 9 — Shares — rights and obligations

Each share confers a proportional right in the company's assets and profits, and one vote at general meetings. Shares are indivisible vis-à-vis the company. Co-owners in indivision must be represented by a single proxy. In the event of usufruct, the voting right belongs to the bare owner for all decisions, except for the allocation of results, where it belongs to the usufructuary.

## TITLE III — TRANSFER AND TRANSMISSION OF SHARES

### Article 10 — Transfer between shareholders

Shares are freely transferable between shareholders, subject to the provisions of the Shareholders' Agreement.

### Article 11 — Transfer to third parties or to a spouse

Any transfer to a third party, a spouse, an ascendant or a descendant of a shareholder is subject to the prior approval of the general meeting. The request is notified by registered letter indicating the identity of the transferee, the number of shares, the price and the conditions.

The general meeting decides within 30 days. The decision is taken by a majority representing at least **50 % of the capital**. The transferor does not take part in the vote. In the event of refusal, the remaining shareholders purchase the shares within 3 months; failing which, the approval is deemed granted.

### Article 12 — Death, incapacity, divorce

In the event of death, the company continues with the surviving shareholders and the heirs, subject to approval. In the event of refusal, the heirs retain the financial rights but without voting rights until the buy-back in accordance with the Shareholders' Agreement.

In the event of divorce or liquidation of the matrimonial regime, the shares allotted to the spouse are subject to approval under the same conditions.

### Article 13 — Opposability of the transfer

The transfer is opposable to the company after notification by registered letter and entry in the share register kept at the registered office.

## TITLE IV — ADMINISTRATION

### Article 14 — Board of directors

The company is administered by a board of directors of one or more directors, appointed by the general meeting. In the event of several directors, they form a college electing a chairman. The parties may replace a director position with an observer position.

### Article 15 — Powers

The board of directors has all powers necessary to achieve the corporate purpose, except those reserved by law or the articles of association to the general meeting. The company is bound by the acts of a director acting alone, even beyond the corporate purpose, except where the third party had knowledge of the excess.

For expenditures exceeding **10 000 euros** in the context of daily management and investment, the expenditure must be authorised in advance by the **signature of 2 directors**. For expenditures exceeding **30 000 euros**, the **prior approval of the board of directors** is required. Depending on the evolution of operations, these ceilings will be revised.

### Article 16 — Daily management

The board may delegate daily management to one or more directors or agents.

### Article 17 — Remuneration

The remuneration of the directors is fixed by the general meeting.

### Article 18 — Regulated transactions

Any transaction between the company and a director having a direct or indirect interest is subject to the prior authorisation of the board, the director concerned not taking part in the vote. These transactions are brought to the attention of the general meeting in the management report.

## TITLE V — CONTROL

### Article 19 — Statutory auditor

The company may appoint a statutory auditor in accordance with the CSA. The appointment is mandatory once the legal thresholds are exceeded. Term of 3 years, renewable.

---

## TITLE VI — GENERAL MEETINGS

### Article 20 — Ordinary general meeting

Annual meeting within 6 months following the close of the financial year. Convened 15 days in advance. Each shareholder may be represented. Decisions by simple majority.

### Article 21 — Extraordinary general meeting

Decides on amendments to the articles of association, the capital and dissolution. Attendance quorum: 50 % of the capital. Decisions by a 3/4 majority.

### Article 22 — Minutes

Signed by the chairman and one shareholder, kept at the registered office in a register of meetings.

---

## TITLE VII — FINANCIAL YEAR, ACCOUNTS, ALLOCATION

### Article 23 — Financial year

The financial year runs from 1 January to 31 December of each year. The first financial year runs from incorporation until 31 December of the following year.

### Article 24 — Annual accounts

The board of directors prepares the inventory, the annual accounts and the management report in accordance with the CSA. They are sent to the shareholders together with the convening notice.

### Article 25 — Allocation of results

Appropriation: 5 % of the net profit to the legal reserve until it reaches 10 % of the share capital. The balance is allocated by the general meeting on the proposal of the board. No distribution may be made if the net assets fall below the capital increased by the unavailable reserves.

---

## TITLE VIII — DISSOLUTION AND LIQUIDATION

### Article 26 — Dissolution

Pronounced by the extraordinary general meeting. Liquidator(s) appointed, powers and remuneration fixed.

### Article 27 — Liquidation

After payment of the debts, the net assets are distributed in proportion to the shares.

---

## TITLE IX — DISPUTES

### Article 28 — Dispute resolution

Any dispute is submitted to the resolution procedure of the Shareholders' Agreement. Failing this, exclusive jurisdiction of the Belgian enterprise courts.
