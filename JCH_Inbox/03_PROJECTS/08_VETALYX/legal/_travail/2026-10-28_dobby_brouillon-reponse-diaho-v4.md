**Subject: Re: 回复：回复：Vetalyx contracts After your comments — Framework Agreement v4 and Articles of Association**

Dear Li Yan, dear all,

Thank you for your reply of 28 October, and for the revised framework agreement (v4) and the R&D progress report. We have reviewed them carefully with our counsel, and we are pleased that we are converging. Please find our response below, point by point.

**1. Framework agreement — governing language and governing law**

We maintain that **English** should be the official language of the framework agreement; Chinese and French remain working versions. This prevents misunderstanding and protects both parties.

On governing law, we understand and accept that the related-party transactions between VETALYX and DIAHO must comply with the **Company Law of the People's Republic of China** — this is a legal requirement for DIAHO, and we fully respect it. We therefore propose:

- **Belgian law governs the framework agreement and its performance** (distribution, exclusivity, intellectual property, execution);
- **PRC Company Law applies to the related-party transactions** between VETALYX and DIAHO, to the extent required for DIAHO's compliance.

This avoids a situation in which two laws govern the entire agreement at once, which would create uncertainty for both of us.

**2. Framework agreement — dispute resolution**

We are unable to accept arbitration before CIETAC in Hangzhou, and we would like to be transparent about the reasons, as this point is essential for us.

VETALYX is a newly incorporated company whose resources are entirely dedicated to launching its operations in Europe. It will never have the financial means to bear the cost of conducting an arbitration in a venue geographically distant from its centre of activity. A dispute that required arbitration in such a distant location would, in practice, be commercially untenable.

We therefore require a **geographically neutral seat**. We propose **ICC arbitration under the ICC Rules of Arbitration, with its seat in Geneva (Switzerland)**, conducted in English (and Chinese where necessary). This is a standard, neutral solution that protects both parties equally, and it is a condition for us to proceed.

**3. Framework agreement — contracting party and guarantee**

We understand and accept that **DIAHO alone** is the contracting party, and that no third party should complicate the main agreement. We agree with this in principle.

However, because the Products are manufactured by DIAHO's partners — in particular Zheda Dixun — and because DIAHO federates and coordinates those partners, we require, as a condition for us to proceed:

- a **guarantee by Zheda Dixun** of DIAHO's obligations under the agreement (as an annex, without making Dixun a party to the main agreement); and
- a **back-to-back agreement between DIAHO and Zheda Dixun**, provided to us before signature, evidencing DIAHO's ability to perform.

This gives both parties direct confidence in the supply chain and protects the commercial relationship.

**4. Articles of association — voting rights and board**

We confirm: DIAHO, holding **40% of the share capital**, holds **40% of the voting rights**, and is entitled to designate **one (1) director** on a board of **three (3) directors** (the two remaining directors being designated by the European founders), as set out in the articles of association and the shareholders' agreement. This is consistent with your understanding.

**5. Articles of association — business scope**

We are open to extending the business scope as you suggest (animal habitat supplies, food, and Class 31 trademarks). Our only condition is that this extension must not create delays in the registration/screening process with the Belgian authorities. We will verify this point with our notary and revert to you promptly.

**6. R&D progress**

Thank you for the detailed R&D progress on the international pet allergy kit. We note the overall completion of approximately 40%, and the open point regarding positive samples for the flea and mosquito projects. Our scientific advisor will review the document and return with any questions.

**Next steps**

We believe we are close to finalising the framework agreement. Once you have reviewed the above, we propose to schedule a call to align on the remaining open points (the Chinese name of DIAHO and the pricing review mechanism).

This message is sent for discussion purposes only and without prejudice to either party's position. Nothing herein constitutes a binding commitment until a signed agreement.

With best regards,

Alain Bosseloir, Jean-Claude Havaux, Jacques Noury, Peiwen Qi
