# Note — Influence of the ISC on the adaptation of the VETALYX articles of association

**Date :** 2026-10-05 | **Orchestrated by :** Dobby 🦉
**Subject :** traceability of the adaptations made to the articles of association to minimise exposure to the Belgian foreign investment screening (ISC).

---

## 1. Reminder — the ISC mechanism

The **ISC** (Interfederal Screening Committee for foreign direct investments, established by the cooperation agreement of 30 November 2022) reviews equity stakes taken by foreign investors in **sensitive sectors**: health, biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, critical infrastructure, media, etc.

The entry of **DIAHO (China) at 40 %** into VETALYX (veterinary diagnostics — a field close to health/biotech) is potentially covered by this mechanism.

## 2. The risk trigger

The ISC risk is **low in itself** (a company with EUR 10 000 of capital, a non-critical activity), **but it increases** if DIAHO obtains **special rights recorded directly in the articles of association** (right of veto, reserved seat on the board) — since « 40 % + special rights = a clearer significant influence », which makes screening more likely to apply.

## 3. Adaptations made to the articles of association (and their reason)

| Adaptation | Article | ISC-related reason |
|---|---|---|
| DIAHO veto **removed from the articles**, kept in the Shareholders' Agreement | (former Art. 21) | Do not disclose special rights in the published document |
| General meeting at a **standard 3/4 majority** (no reference to the Shareholders' Agreement) | Art. 21 | Remove the statutory anchor of the veto |
| No mention of a **DIAHO reserved seat** on the board | Art. 14, 33 | The board remains standard (directors appointed by the general meeting) |
| No majority « including the DIAHO vote » | — | No special rights visible in the articles |
| Expenditure in **2 tiers** (EUR 10 000 / 2 directors, EUR 30 000 / board) | Art. 15 | Standard management rule, neutral for the ISC |

## 4. Accepted consequence

Since the DIAHO veto is no longer in the articles of association, it becomes **not opposable to third parties** (it binds only the shareholders, via the Shareholders' Agreement). This is the accepted trade-off: **ISC discretion > opposability of the veto**. To be documented with the notary, with voluntary notification possible in case of doubt (timelines: phase 1 = 30 days, phase 2 = +28 days).
