## TITLE I --- FORM, NAME, REGISTERED OFFICE, DURATION, PURPOSE第一部分---形式、名称、注册办公地、期限、目的

### Article 1 --- Form第1条------形式

The company is incorporated as a Belgian private limited liability
company (*société à responsabilité limitée*, SRL), governed by the
Companies and Associations Code
(CSA).公司设立为比利时私人有限责任公司（société à responsabilité
limitée，SRL），受《公司与协会法典》（CSA）管辖

### Article 2 --- Name第2条------名称

The company is named « VETALYX ». In all deeds, invoices, notices,
publications and other documents issued by the company, the corporate
name must be preceded or followed by the indication « SRL », together
with the registered office and the company number with the Crossroads
Bank for Enterprises
(BCE).公司名称为"VETALYX"。在公司签发的所有契据、发票、通知、公告和其他文件中，公司名称之前或之后必须标明"SRL"，并附注册办公地和企业在跨部门银行（BCE）的公司编号。

### Article 3 --- Registered office第3条------注册办公地

The registered office is established at Novalis Science Park, Rue de la
Science, 6900 Marche-en-Famenne.注册办公地设立于Novalis Science Park,
Rue de la Science, 6900 Marche-en-Famenne。

It may be transferred to any other place in the Kingdom by decision of
the board of directors, without amendment of these articles of
association.注册办公地可由董事会决定迁至王国境内任何其他地点，无需修改公司章程。

### Article 4 --- Duration第4条------期限

The company is incorporated for an unlimited duration. It may be
dissolved at any time by decision of the extraordinary general
meeting.公司设立期限为无限期。可随时由临时股东大会决定解散

### Article 9 --- Shares --- rights and obligations第9条---股份---权利和义务

Each share confers a proportional right in the company's assets and
profits, and one vote at general meetings. Shares are indivisible
vis-à-vis the company. Co-owners in indivision must be represented by a
single proxy. In the event of usufruct, the voting right belongs to the
bare owner for all decisions, except for the allocation of results,
where it belongs to the
usufructuary.每股在公司资产和利润中赋予按比例的权利，并在股东大会上赋予一票表决权。就公司而言，股份不可分割。不可分割的共有人必须由单一代理人代表。在用益权情形下，表决权属于虚有所有权人，但利润分配事项除外，该事项的表决权属于用益权人

## TITLE III --- TRANSFER AND TRANSMISSION OF SHARES第三部分---股份的转让和传承

### Article 10 --- Transfer between shareholders第10条------股东之间的转让

Shares are freely transferable between shareholders, subject to the
provisions of the Shareholders'
Agreement.股份在股东之间可自由转让，但须遵守股东协议的规定

### Article 11 --- Transfer to third parties or to a spouse第11条------向第三方或配偶的转让

Any transfer to a third party, a spouse, an ascendant or a descendant of
a shareholder is subject to the prior approval of the general meeting.
The request is notified by registered letter indicating the identity of
the transferee, the number of shares, the price and the
conditions.向股东以外的第三方、配偶、直系尊亲属或直系卑亲属的任何转让，均须经股东大会事先批准。请求应以挂号信通知，说明受让人的身份、股份数量、价格和条件

The general meeting decides within 30 days. The decision is taken by a
majority representing at least **50 % of the capital**. The transferor
does not take part in the vote. In the event of refusal, the remaining
shareholders purchase the shares within 3 months; failing which, the
approval is deemed
granted.股东大会在30日内决定。决定须由代表至少50%资本的多数作出。转让方不参加表决。如拒绝，其余股东在3个月内购买该股份；否则，视为批准

### Article 12 --- Death, incapacity, divorce第12条------死亡、无行为能力、离婚

In the event of death, the company continues with the surviving
shareholders and the heirs, subject to approval. In the event of
refusal, the heirs retain the financial rights but without voting rights
until the buy-back in accordance with the Shareholders'
Agreement.在死亡情况下，公司继续与存续股东和继承人存续，但须经批准。如拒绝，继承人在依股东协议回购之前保留财务权利，但不享有表决权

In the event of divorce or liquidation of the matrimonial regime, the
shares allotted to the spouse are subject to approval under the same
conditions.在离婚或婚姻财产制清算的情况下，分配给配偶的股份须按相同条件批准

### Article 13 --- Opposability of the transfer第13条------转让的可对抗性

The transfer is opposable to the company after notification by
registered letter and entry in the share register kept at the registered
office.转让在挂号信通知并记入注册办公地保存的股份登记册后，可对抗公司

## TITLE IV --- ADMINISTRATION第四部分---行政

### Article 14 --- Board of directors第14条------董事会

The company is administered by a board of directors of one or more
directors, appointed by the general meeting. In the event of several
directors, they form a college electing a chairman. The parties may
replace a director position with an observer
position.公司由一名或多名董事组成的董事会管理，董事由股东大会任命。在有多名董事的情况下，他们组成合议机构并选举一名主席。各方可将董事职位替换为观察员职位

### Article 15 --- Powers第15条------权力

The board of directors has all powers necessary to achieve the corporate
purpose, except those reserved by law or the articles of association to
the general meeting. The company is bound by the acts of a director
acting alone, even beyond the corporate purpose, except where the third
party had knowledge of the
excess.董事会拥有实现公司目的所需的所有权力，但法律或公司章程保留给股东大会的权力除外。公司受单独行事的董事行为的约束，即使超出公司目的，但第三方知悉该越权的情形除外

For expenditures exceeding **10 000 euros** in the context of daily
management and investment, the expenditure must be authorised in advance
by the **signature of 2 directors**. For expenditures exceeding **30 000
euros**, the **prior approval of the board of directors** is required.
Depending on the evolution of operations, these ceilings will be
revised.对于日常管理和投资中超过10,000欧元的支出，该支出须经2名董事签字事先授权。对于超过30,000欧元的支出，须经董事会事先批准。根据运营的发展，这些上限将被修订

### Article 16 --- Daily management第16条------日常管理

The board may delegate daily management to one or more directors or
agents.董事会可将日常管理委托给一名或多名董事或代理人

### Article 17 --- Remuneration第17条------报酬

The remuneration of the directors is fixed by the general
meeting.董事的报酬由股东大会确定

### Article 18 --- Regulated transactions第18条------受监管交易

Any transaction between the company and a director having a direct or
indirect interest is subject to the prior authorisation of the board,
the director concerned not taking part in the vote. These transactions
are brought to the attention of the general meeting in the management
report.公司与有直接或间接利益的董事之间的任何交易，须经董事会事先授权，相关董事不参加表决。这些交易在管理报告中提请股东大会注意

## TITLE VI --- GENERAL MEETINGS第六部分---股东大会

### Article 20 --- Ordinary general meeting第20条------普通股东大会

Annual meeting within 6 months following the close of the financial
year. Convened 15 days in advance. Each shareholder may be represented.
Decisions by simple
majority.在财务年度结束后6个月内召开年度会议。提前15日召集。每名股东可被代表。以简单多数决定

### Article 21 --- Extraordinary general meeting第21条------临时股东大会

Decides on amendments to the articles of association, the capital and
dissolution. Attendance quorum: 50 % of the capital. Decisions by a 3/4
majority.就公司章程修改、资本和解散作出决定。出席法定人数：资本的50%。以3/4多数决定

### Article 22 --- Minutes第22条------会议记录

Signed by the chairman and one shareholder, kept at the registered
office in a register of
meetings.由主席和一名股东签署，保存在注册办公地的会议登记册中

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## TITLE VII --- FINANCIAL YEAR, ACCOUNTS, ALLOCATION第七部分---财务年度、账目、分配

### Article 23 --- Financial year第23条------财务年度

The financial year runs from 1 January to 31 December of each year. The
first financial year runs from incorporation until 31 December of the
following
year.财务年度为每年1月1日至12月31日。首个财务年度自设立起至次年12月31日

### Article 24 --- Annual accounts第24条------年度账目

The board of directors prepares the inventory, the annual accounts and
the management report in accordance with the CSA. They are sent to the
shareholders together with the convening
notice.董事会依CSA编制盘点、年度账目和管理报告。它们与召集通知一并送交股东

### Article 25 --- Allocation of results第25条------结果分配

Appropriation: 5 % of the net profit to the legal reserve until it
reaches 10 % of the share capital. The balance is allocated by the
general meeting on the proposal of the board. No distribution may be
made if the net assets fall below the capital increased by the
unavailable
reserves.拨用：净利的5%拨入法定公积金，直至达到股本的10%。余额由股东大会根据董事会的提议分配。如净资产低于资本加上不可用公积金，不得分配

## TITLE VIII --- DISSOLUTION AND LIQUIDATION第八部分---解散和清算

### Article 26 --- Dissolution第26条------解散

Pronounced by the extraordinary general meeting. Liquidator(s)
appointed, powers and remuneration
fixed.由临时股东大会宣布。任命清算人，确定其权力和报酬

### Article 27 --- Liquidation第27条------清算
