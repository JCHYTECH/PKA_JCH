## Slide 3 — Le manuscrit qui résiste

**Surtitre :** *The manuscript that resists* (au lieu de « EPISTEMOLOGY OF DECIPHERMENT »)

- **The Voynich enigma :** Six hundred years, two hundred pages, plants that do not exist, a script in no known language. The word distribution is not random — it follows rules close to a natural language. And yet: no translation.
- **The rule :** AI decodes only when the language is known and the corpus is large. No Rosetta Stone, no translation.
- **The counter-example :** Herculaneum 2026 — the real AI success. Scrolls carbonized by Vesuvius, read for the first time in two thousand years. But they were Greek, a language we already read. The machine made the ink legible; it did not translate.

---

## Slide 4 — Le même mur, côté animal

**Surtitre :** *The same wall, in the animal world* (au lieu de « BIOACOUSTICS & COGNITION »)

*(Garde la phrase « Structure is cheap. Meaning is not. » — c'est le point.)*

- **The whale :** In 2024, a study proposed a « phonetic alphabet » for sperm whales. The structure is there — repeated, measurable patterns. The meaning stays closed. It is the Voynich in acoustic form.
- **The toddler test :** In 2026, AI was fed the vocalizations of toddlers — sounds we understand. It grouped together different meanings, and split apart identical ones. Patterns, not significance.

---

## Slide 5 — Le cerveau ne suffit pas

**Surtitre :** *The brain is not enough* (au lieu de « NEUROBIOLOGY VS SEMANTICS »)

- **The finch :** In the zebra finch, researchers rebuilt the song from neural activity alone — and decoded its dreams: asleep, it sings melodies it never sings awake.
- **The boundary :** But that is motor production, not meaning. Knowing how a sound is made says nothing about what it means. You can read the score without hearing the music.

---

## Slide 6 — La fleur qu'on ne voit pas

**Surtitre :** *The flower we cannot see* (au lieu de « SENSORY APPARATUS & WORLDS »)

- **The UV guides :** The bee sees ultraviolet landing strips on a flower that, to us, is just white. We photograph the world we perceive — and we perceive little.
- **The hearing slit :** Humans hear 20 Hz – 20 kHz. Bats reach 120 kHz, elephants drop to 5 Hz. For decades we missed rodent ultrasound and elephant infrasound, because our instruments stopped where our ears did.
- **Beyond sound :** Chemistry, electricity, magnetism, vibration. Each is a wavelength we might forget — like analyzing a flower in RGB and never switching to ultraviolet.

---

## Slide 7 — L'IA peut-elle déduire un umwelt inconnu ?

**Surtitre :** *Can AI deduce an unknown world?* (au lieu de « EPISTEMOLOGICAL LOOP »)

- **No sensor, no data :** An AI cannot train on a signal that never touched an instrument.
- **The residue :** But it can point to what is missing — the way we found exoplanets by anomaly, and dark matter by an unexplained residue.
- **The loop :** Measure wide → detect the gap → hypothesize a channel → build the sensor → begin again. AI is a link in the loop, not the engine.

---

## Slide 8 — Ce que les anciens savaient (et l'indicateur)

**Surtitre :** *What the ancients knew* (au lieu de « MYTHOLOGY & FIELD COOPERATION »)

- **The old dream :** The Quran says Solomon was « taught the language of the birds ». Attar wrote the Conference of the Birds; Gongye Chang understood them in China; the yogis spoke of a power to know the sounds of all beings. The dream is old.
- **The real thing :** The greater honeyguide and the Yao honey-hunters. The call « brrr-hm », passed from father to son, triples the odds the bird will lead them to wild honey. This is not translation — it is mutualism: two species sharing a signal, neither decoding the other.

---

## Slide 9 — La loi du dialecte

**Surtitre :** *The law of dialect* (au lieu de « EVOLUTIONARY BIOACOUSTICS »)

*(Garde les 4 blocs et la citation « Dialect is the luxury of non-vital signals. » — c'est le cœur.)*

- **The convergent « seet » :** Unrelated birds share the same high whistle, because a predator cannot locate it. Under threat, the dialect does not just fade — it fuses across species.

---

## Slide 10 — Trois leçons

**Surtitre :** *Three lessons* (au lieu de « SYNTHESIS & PRINCIPLES »)

- **1. AI is a pattern machine, not a meaning machine.** It can map the Voynich, the whale's alphabet, the finch's dreams. It cannot cross from pattern to meaning without a shared world.
- **2. Our umwelt is a trap.** We search for animal language with human ears and human categories. The bee's flower does not exist in our spectrum.
- **3. Meaning is not decoded — it is shared.** The Yao hunter and the honeyguide do not translate one another. Over generations, they learned to inhabit the same signal.
