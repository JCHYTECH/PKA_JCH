## Slide 1 — AI Will Never Speak the Language of the Living

*From the Voynich Manuscript to birdsong — why the machine sees patterns, not meaning*

**Note orateur :** Opening. The thesis in one line: the machine sees patterns; meaning requires a shared world.

---

## Slide 2 — Every two years, the same headline
- “AI has deciphered the Voynich Manuscript.”
- Every claim is rejected by scholars.
- 2018: “Hebrew encoded as anagrams” — collapsed.
- 2026: still undeciphered.

**Note orateur :** The Voynich is not the story — it is the symptom of a deeper limit.

---

## Slide 3 — A real book, no known language
- 600 years old, 200 pages.
- Plants that do not exist.
- A script that belongs to no known language.
- AI maps the structure perfectly — no translation.

**Visuel :** Voynich manuscript page

**Note orateur :** The word distribution is not random; it follows rules close to a natural language. Yet no meaning.

---

## Slide 4 — The rule: known language + large corpus
- Linear A, Rongorongo, Indus, Proto-Elamite, Phaistos — all “analyzed”, none decoded.
- AI decodes only when the language is known and the corpus is large.
- Herculaneum (June 2026): an entire scroll read for the first time.
- But those were Greek texts — a language we already read.

**Visuel :** Herculaneum scroll (X-ray unwrap)

**Note orateur :** Herculaneum is the real AI success. But it did not translate — it made legible what the ink had erased.

---

## Slide 5 — Structure is cheap. Meaning is not.
- Sperm whale “phonetic alphabet” (2024): structure, no meaning.
- Project CETI: massive data, no translation.
- It is the Voynich in acoustic form.

**Visuel :** Sperm whale song spectrogram

**Note orateur :** A larger corpus does not help. What is missing is a key — a verifiable link between signal and referent.

---

## Slide 6 — AI confuses meaning (Tel Aviv, 2026)
- Tested on toddler vocalizations — a language we partly understand.
- AI grouped sounds of different meaning together.
- AI separated sounds of identical meaning.
- Patterns, not significance.

**Note orateur :** Even with a language we understand, the network sees patterns, not meaning.

---

## Slide 7 — The brain is not enough
- Zebra finch: song reconstructed from neural activity alone.
- One lab decodes their dreams — different songs asleep.
- But this is motor production, not meaning.
- You can read the score without hearing the music.

**Visuel :** Zebra finch + HVC brain schematic

**Note orateur :** Knowing how a sound is made says nothing about what it means.

---

## Slide 8 — The flower we never see
- Bees see ultraviolet guides that we do not see.
- Humans hear 20 Hz – 20 kHz.
- Bat: 120 kHz. Dolphin: 150 kHz. Elephant: 5 Hz.
- We missed rodent ultrasound and elephant infrasound for decades.

**Visuel :** UV flower (bee vision)

**Note orateur :** The umwelt — the perceptual world proper to each species (Jakob von Uexküll).

---

## Slide 9 — Sound is only one axis
- Chemistry — pheromones.
- Electricity — electric fish “speak” in fields.
- Magnetism — bird navigation.
- Substrate vibration — spiders.
- Each is a wavelength we might forget — RGB without UV.

**Note orateur :** Our own senses are a trap: we search for animal “language” with human ears, eyes, categories.

---

## Slide 10 — Can AI deduce an unknown umwelt?
- No sensor → no data → no inference.
- AI cannot guess a signal no instrument has measured.
- Bird magnetoreception was found by experiment, not analysis.

**Note orateur :** AI works on data. It cannot invent a channel from nothing.

---

## Slide 11 — But AI can signal a missing channel
- Exoplanets: found by anomaly, not by sight.
- Dark matter: an unexplained residue.
- AI detects unexplained correlation → predicts an umwelt.
- The loop: measure → detect residue → hypothesize → build a sensor.

**Visuel :** Discovery loop diagram

**Note orateur :** That is a prediction, not a discovery. Characterizing the medium remains physics and sensors.

---

## Slide 12 — The dream is old
- Quran 27:16 — Solomon “taught the language of the birds”.
- Attar, The Conference of the Birds (1177).
- Gongye Chang (China) understood the birds.
- Hindu siddhi — “knowledge of the sounds of all beings”.
- Shamanism — the animal as teacher.

**Note orateur :** The desire to speak with the living is universal — older than the algorithms.

---

## Slide 13 — The real version: not translation, mutualism
- Greater honeyguide + Yao honey-hunters (Mozambique).
- The “brrr-hm” call, passed from father to son.
- The bird triples the odds of being guided.
- Spottiswoode proved the bird recognizes the exact call.
- Co-evolutionary shared signaling — neither “decodes” the other.

**Visuel :** Honeyguide + Yao honey-hunter

**Note orateur :** This is the living answer to Solomon: meaning is shared, not decoded.

---

## Slide 14 — Dialect disappears before the predator
- Song is learned → dialects.
- Alarm calls are innate → conserved.
- Facing a predator, selection freezes the signal.
- The “seet” call is identical across unrelated species.

**Visuel :** “Seet” call spectrogram, convergent

**Note orateur :** Marler's localization hypothesis (1955): a high, narrow sound is impossible for a predator to localize.

---

## Slide 15 — The law, in one sentence

> “Dialect is a luxury of signals that are not life-or-death.”

The moment a signal becomes a matter of survival, selection freezes it — and fuses it across species.

**Note orateur :** Reconfirmed by the Cornell Lab, October 2025.

---

## Slide 16 — Three things, which are one
- AI is a pattern machine, not a meaning machine.
- Our own umwelt is a trap.
- Meaning is not decoded — it is shared.

**Note orateur :** The through-line of the whole essay.

---

## Slide 17 — Look long enough, precisely enough

> “The machine maps what is measurable. The meaning belongs to the one who stays still, listens — and is willing to be looked back at.”

WildLens — understanding the living is not calculation, but belonging.
