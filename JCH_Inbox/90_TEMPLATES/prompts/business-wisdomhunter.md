## A. Revenu passif / business en ligne (5 prompts)

### A1. Build my passive income system
> Act as a passive income strategist. Based on my skills in [YOUR SKILLS], budget of [$AMOUNT], and available time of [HOURS PER DAY], build a realistic online income system that can eventually generate recurring revenue. Prioritize digital products, AI automation, affiliate marketing, or other suitable models. Give me a step-by-step launch plan, free tools, monetization strategy, and a 30-day execution roadmap.

### A2. Find my most profitable income idea
> Analyze my interests, skills, and resources: [YOUR DETAILS]. Identify 5 online business ideas with the potential to generate recurring income without requiring constant daily involvement. For each idea, explain the target audience, problem solved, startup cost, revenue model, competition, and first steps. Rank them by ease of execution and realistic earning potential.

### A3. Create a digital product that sells automatically
> Act as a digital product expert. Help me create a valuable digital product for [YOUR NICHE] that solves [SPECIFIC PROBLEM]. Choose the best format, develop the product outline, suggest a competitive price, and create a compelling offer. Then design an automated sales funnel using a landing page, free lead magnet, email sequence, and checkout system so customers can discover, purchase, and receive the product with minimal manual work.

### A4. Automate my online business with AI
> Act as an AI automation specialist. Design a system for my [BUSINESS TYPE] that automates repetitive tasks using free or affordable AI tools. Include content creation, lead generation, customer follow-ups, sales, payment processing, and product delivery wherever appropriate. Recommend specific tools, explain how to connect them, and provide a beginner-friendly implementation guide. Prioritize reliable workflows that require minimal ongoing maintenance.

### A5. Build my 90-day passive income action plan
> Create a practical 90-day roadmap to build an online income stream around [MY BUSINESS IDEA]. I can invest [BUDGET] and dedicate [HOURS PER DAY]. Divide the plan into weekly milestones covering product development, audience building, marketing, automation, and sales optimization. Set measurable targets, identify the most important daily actions, and explain how to reinvest early profits. Include realistic revenue scenarios and contingency plans without assuming guaranteed earnings.

## B. Produits numériques depuis une compétence (7 prompts)

### B1. Profitable ideas from your skill (15 produits)
> Act as a digital product strategist. My skill is [skill], my level is [beginner, intermediate, or expert], and my audience is [audience]. Give me 15 ideas for passive-income products based on this skill. For each one, include what it is, who buys it, what problem it solves, how difficult it is to create, how quickly I can launch it, and the best platform to sell it on. Rank them from the easiest money to the highest long-term potential.

### B2. Products for beginners (10 produits simples)
> Help me turn my skill in [skill] into simple digital products I can sell online. My target audience is [audience]. Suggest 10 products that don't require advanced technical skills and that I can create using tools like Google Docs, Canva, Notion, or spreadsheets. For each product, provide a name, format, price range, creation steps, and explain why people would buy it.

### B3. Product ladder (4 niveaux)
> Act as an expert in product ecosystems. My skill is [skill] and my audience is [audience]. Build a 4-level product ladder: low-ticket, mid-ticket, premium digital product, and recurring offer. Each offer should naturally connect to the next. For each level, explain what to sell, the price range, delivery format, type of buyer, and how it leads to the next product.

### B4. Launch in 7 days
> I want a passive income product that I can create and launch in 7 days using my skill in [skill]. I can dedicate [X] hours per day and my target audience is [audience]. Give me 7 product ideas that can be created quickly, and for each one include a step-by-step daily plan, tools, estimated price, difficulty level, and the best option for speed, simplicity, and first sales.

### B5. A sellable mini-course
> Act as an instructional designer with expertise in digital products. My field is [topic] and my target audience is [audience profile]. Create the complete structure for a 5-module mini-course that I can sell for between $47 and $97: a compelling title, the outline, the content of each module, practical deliverables, and 3 ways to get the first 10 buyers without paid advertising.

### B6. Validate before creating
> Act as a market validation researcher. I want to sell a passive income product based on [skill] to [audience]. Give me 10 ideas and rate each one from 1 to 10 based on demand, urgency, ease of creation, uniqueness, and probability of purchase. Then tell me the 3 with the highest probability of generating fast income and why.

### B7. What you already know
> I don't want to start from scratch. Help me turn what I already know about [skill] into digital products. Ask me what tasks I repeat, what I explain often, what tools I use, what mistakes I help others avoid, and what methods I already follow. Then turn it into 10 passive income product ideas with clear format, title ideas, and ideal customers.
