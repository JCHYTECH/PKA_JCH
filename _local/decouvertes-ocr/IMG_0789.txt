Embark on a tour of Germany's most magnificent castles, each brimming with
history, legends, and architectural splendor. Swipe left to uncover the magic of
these historical treasures. 2. jay

* **Albrechtsburg Castle**: Majestic and serene, Albrechtsburg is renowned
as Germany's oldest palace, set against the backdrop of the Elbe valley.

* **Eltz Castle**: Nestled in a forest, Eltz Castle is a medieval wonder,
untouched by time, famous for its beauty and family ownership spanning over
800 years.

* **Lichtenstein Castle**: Perched on a cliff, Lichtenstein is a 19th-century
Gothic Revival castle, offering breathtaking views and a fairy-tale atmosphere.

* **Cochem Castle**: Overlooking the Moselle River, Cochem castle is a
hilltop medieval fortification, restored to its former glory, showcasing stunning
views and rich history.

* **Neuschwanstein Castle**: The inspiration for Disney's Cinderella Castle,
Neuschwanstein is a dreamy 19th-century palace, embodying the romantic
spirit of the era.

* **Hohenzollern Castle**: An ancestral seat of the Hohenzollern family, this
fortress stands majestically atop Mount Hohenzollern, enveloped by forests
and panoramic vistas.

* **Drachenburg Castle**: A relatively modern castle near the Rhine,
Drachenburg combines opulent interiors with picturesque gardens, resembling
a setting from a storybook.

* **Castle of Fussen**: Overlooking the town of Fussen, this castle offers a
blend of historical architecture and captivating natural scenery.
