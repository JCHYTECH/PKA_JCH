thealternativeguide_ The Golden Chamber,
St. Ursula's Basilica | Cologne ~ Save for
future trips

The Golden Chamber is a 17th-century
reliquary built to house remains uncovered from
the Roman cemetery beneath the basilica. The
room is small but magnificent, its walls lined
with human bones fixed into complex patterns
and Latin inscriptions that reflect the devotional
practices of the period.
